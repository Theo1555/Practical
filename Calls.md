# Introduction
WhatsApp allows you to make video & voice calls directly from your laptop. This section explains how to start and manage both video & voice calls.

## Prerequisite
   * Your laptop has a working microphone
   * WhatsApp has permission to use your microphone 
   * Your laptop must have stable network

## Making Voice Call
1. Open WhatsApp.
2. Open the chat with the person you want to call.
3. Click the phone icon at the top of the chat.
4. WhatsApp will start the voice call.

## Making Video Call
1. Open WhatsApp.
2. Open the chat with the person you want to call.
3. Click the video camera icon at the top of the chat.
4. WhatsApp will start the call.

## Switching Between Voice and Video Calls
1. Start or answer a voice call.
2. During the call, tap the camera icon.
3. WhatsApp will send a request to switch the call to video.
4. Once the other person accepts, the video will turn on.

## Switching Between Video and Voice Calls
1. Start or answer a video call.
2. During the call, tap the camera icon to turn off your camera.
3. The call will continue with audio while your camera remains off.
