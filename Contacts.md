# Introduction 
WhatsApp allows you to manage your contacts directly from the desktop application. This section provides a step-by-step guide to finding, viewing and  managing contacts on WhatsApp.

## Adding A New Contact
1. Open WhatsApp.
2. Select New Chat. 
3. Select New Contact.
4. Enter the contact's details.
5. Click Save.

<img width="700" height="408" alt="Open the chat with the contact (9)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/014b806a-19e5-44a7-9a89-a96a94e361b4" />


The new contact will appear in your WhatsApp contact list.

## Searching for a Contact
1. Open WhatsApp.
2. Click the search bar.
3. Enter the contact's name or phone number.
4. Select the contact from the search results.

Your WhatsApp chat with the selected contact will open.

## Viewing Contact Information
1. Open WhatsApp.
2. Open the chat with contact.
3. Click the contact name at the top of the chat.
4. View the available contact information.

## Editing Contact Information
1. Open the chat with the contact.
2. Click the contact's name.
3. Select Edit Contact.
4. Update the necessary information.
5. Click Save.


<img width="700" height="408" alt="Open the chat with the contact (11)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f6a775c5-0e65-4a7b-a39f-6d7005d4e133" />


## Blocking a Contact
1. Open the chat with the contact.
2. Click the contact's name.
3. Scroll down until you see the Block contact option.
4. Select Block contact.

<img width="412" height="317" alt="Screenshot 2026-08-22 001230" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/aa45c1b0-c840-4223-b878-ad6f5699d263" />




The WhatsApp contact will be unable to message or call you on WhatsApp.

## Unblocking a Contact
1. Open Settings.
2. Select Privacy.
3. Open Blocked Contacts.
4. Select the contact you want to unblock.
5. Click Unblock.

<img width="700" height="408" alt="Open the chat with the contact (12)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c58bbfef-d392-4328-a53a-756074974760" />


## Deleting a Contact
1. Open the chat with the contact.
2. Click the contact's name.
3. Select Delete Contact.
4. Confirm the deletion if prompted.

Deleting a contact removes them from your device's contact list but does not automatically delete your chat history.
