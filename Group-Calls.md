# Group Voice and Video Calls
WhatsApp allows you to make voice and video calls with multiple people at the same time. This is useful for group discussions, meetings, or catching up with friends and family.

## Start a Group Call from a Group Chat 
1. Open the WhatsApp group.
2. Click on either the phone or camera icon.
3. The group call starts, and other members can join.
4. Other members can see that the call is in progress and join the call if they want.

## Add People During a Call 
1. During the call, click the (⋮) More options icon
2. Click the Add participant option.
3. Select the contact you want to invite.
4. The person will receive an invitation to join the call.

## Receiving a Call While Already on Another Call

WhatsApp can notify you when someone calls while you are already on another WhatsApp call. You can decide whether to end your current call and answer the new one or decline the incoming call.

## When You Receive an Incoming Call

While you are on a WhatsApp call, an incoming call notification may give you options such as:

* **End & Accept:** End your current call and answer the new call.
* **Decline:** Reject the incoming call and continue your current call.

## Switch to the New Call

### To answer the new call:

1. When the incoming call notification appears, select **End & Accept**.
2. Your current call will end.
3. WhatsApp will connect you to the incoming caller.

### Decline the Incoming Call

If you want to continue your current conversation:

1. When the incoming call notification appears, select **Decline**.
2. The incoming call will be rejected.
3. Your current call will continue.

> **Note:** WhatsApp does not normally place one WhatsApp-to-WhatsApp call on hold while you answer another. Your available options may vary depending on the type of call and your WhatsApp version.
