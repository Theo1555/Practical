# Introduction
WhatsApp allows users to create and manage group conversations with multiple contacts. This section provides a step-by-step guide to creating, managing, and participating in WhatsApp groups.

## Creating a Group
1. Open WhatsApp and select New Chat.
2. Select New Group.
3. Select the contacts you want to add.
4. Enter a group name and add a group photo if needed.
5. Click Create.

## Adding a Member to a Group
1. Open the group chat.
2. Click the group name at the top.
3. Select Add Members.
4. Select the contact you want to add.
5. Confirm the action.

## Removing a Member from a Group
1. Open the group chat.
2. Click the group name.
3. Find the member you want to remove.
4. Select the member.
5. Choose Remove from Group.
6. Confirm the action.

## Leaving a Group
1. Open the group chat.
2. Click the group name.
3. Scroll down and select Exit Group.
4. Confirm that you want to leave the group.

## Managing Group Settings
1. Open the group chat.
2. Click the group name.
3. Scroll to the Group Permissions or Group Settings section.
4. Adjust the available settings according to your preference.
