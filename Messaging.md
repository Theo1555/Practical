# Sending Messages
WhatsApp provides several ways to communicate with other users. This section guides you through sending messages on WhatsApp using written text, emojis, GIFs, stickers, and voice messages.

## Steps
1. Open the chat with the contact.
2. Click the message field.
3. Type your message.
4. Press Enter or click the Send button.

# Sending Voice Message 
WhatsApp allows you to communicate using voice messages when typing isn't convenient. This section explains how to record and send a voice message using WhatsApp on your laptop.

## PRerequisite 
Before you begin, make sure:

   * You have an active WhatsApp chat open.
   * Your laptop has a working microphone.
   * WhatsApp has permission to access your microphone.

## Steps
1. Open the chat of the contact you want to send a voice message to. 
2. Click on the microphone icon beside the message text box.
3. Speak your message, then click the send icon when you're finished.

# Using GIFs and Stickers 
WhatsApp allows the use of GIFs and stickers to make messaging more expressive. This section explains how to find and send GIFs and stickers using WhatsApp on your laptop

## Steps 
1. Open the chat where you want to send a GIF or sticker.
2. Click the emoji icon beside the message field.
3. Select GIF or Stickers from the available options.
4. Search for or select the GIF or sticker you want to send.
5. Click the GIF or sticker to send it.

### Expected result:

The selected GIF or sticker appears in the chat and is sent to the recipient.

# Replying and Forwarding Messages
 WhatsApp allows you to reply directly to a specific message or forward a message to another chat. This section explains how to use both features on your laptop.

## Steps — Replying to a message

1. Hover over the message you want to reply to.
2. Click the dropdown arrow that appears beside the message.
3. Select Reply.
4. Type your response and press Enter to send it.

## Steps — Forwarding a message

1. Hover over the message you want to forward.
2. Click the dropdown arrow beside the message.
3. Select Forward.
4. Select the contact or group you want to forward the message to, then click Send.


# Searching for Messages
WhatsApp's search feature allows you to quickly find specific messages within your conversations. This section explains how to search for messages on your laptop.

## Steps

1. Click the Search icon at the top of the chat list.
2. Enter a keyword or phrase from the message you're looking for.
3. Select a result to open the conversation and view the message.

Tip: Use a specific keyword or phrase to narrow down your search results.
