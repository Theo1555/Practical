# Introduction
WhatsApp provides privacy and security settings that allow users to control who can contact them and who can view their information. This section provides a step-by-step guide to managing key privacy and security settings.

## Opening Privacy Settings

1. Open WhatsApp.
2. Click Settings.
3. Select Privacy.

## Managing Last Seen and Online Status

1. Open Privacy settings.
2. Select Last Seen and Online.
3. Choose who can see your last seen and online status.
4. Select your preferred settings.

## Managing Status Privacy

1. Open Privacy settings.
2. Select Status.
3. Choose who can view your status updates.
4. Select your preferred audience.

## Managing Blocked Contacts

1. Open Privacy settings.
2. Select Blocked Contacts.
3. Select Add to block a contact.
4. Select the contact you want to block.

## Enabling Two-Step Verification

1. Open Settings.
2. Select Account.
3. Select Two-Step Verification.
4. Click Enable.
5. Create a six-digit PIN.
6. Confirm the PIN.

Two-step verification adds a layer of security to your WhatsApp account.
