# Introduction 
WhatsApp allows you to share photos and videos directly from your computer with individual contacts or groups. This section aims to provide a step-by-step guide to sharing photos and videos with your WhatsApp contacts or groups.

## Sending a Photo or Video
1. Open WhatsApp.
2. Open the chat with the contact or group you want to send the media to.
3. Click the + (Attach) button beside the message box.
4. Select the photo or video.
5. Add a caption if needed.
6. Click Send.

## Sending Multiple Photos or Videos
You can send several photos or videos at the same time.
1. Open the chat with the contact or group you want to send the media to.
2. Click the + (Attach) button beside the message box.
3. Select a photo or video.
4. Select multiple files on your laptop.
5. Add a caption if needed.
6. Click Send.

WhatsApp will send the selected media together in the chat.

## Adding a Caption

Before sending a photo or video, you can add a caption to provide context or describe the media.

1. Select the photo or video you want to send.
2. Enter your caption in the **Add a caption** field.
3. Click **Send**.

The caption will appear alongside the media in the conversation.

## Dragging and Dropping Media

WhatsApp supports drag-and-drop for photos and videos.

1. Open the chat with the contact or group you want to send the media to.
2. Open File Explorer on your laptop
3. Select the photo or video you want to send.
4. Drag and drop the photo or video into the WhatsApp chat.

## Downloading Received Photo or Video
1. Open the WhatsApp chat containing the photo or video.
2. Click on the download icon on the received Photo or video.
3. The download will begin.
4. Choose where to save the file if prompted.

## Sending Photos as Documents

If you want to send a photo without WhatsApp applying its usual media compression, you can send the image as a document.

1. Open the desired chat.
2. Click the + (Attach) button.
3. Select Document.
4. Browse to the image file on your computer.
5. Select the file.
6. Click Send.

## Deleting Shared Media

You can delete photos or videos that you have sent in a chat.

1. Locate the photo or video.
2. Open the message options.
3. Select Delete.
4. Choose the appropriate deletion option.

Depending on the message and available options, you may be able to delete the media for yourself or for everyone in the conversation.
