# Introduction
This section provides solutions to common problems users may experience while using WhatsApp on a laptop.


## Prerequisites

Before troubleshooting WhatsApp on your laptop, ensure that:

* You have a stable internet connection.
* WhatsApp is installed and updated on your laptop.
* Your phone is connected to the internet.
* You are logged into your WhatsApp account.

## WhatsApp Is Not Connecting
1. Check your internet connection.
2. Restart WhatsApp.
3. Make sure your phone has an active internet connection.

Update WhatsApp if an update is available.


## Messages Are Not Sending
1. Check your internet connection.
2. Restart WhatsApp.
3. Check whether the issue affects other chats.
4. Try sending the message again.


## Calls Are Not Working
1. Check your internet connection.
2. Make sure WhatsApp has permission to access your microphone and camera.
3. Check that the correct microphone and camera are selected.
4. Restart WhatsApp and try the call again.

## WhatsApp Is Not Responding
1. Close WhatsApp.
2. Reopen the application.
3. Restart your laptop if the problem continues.
4. Update WhatsApp to the latest version.
