# Introduction
WhatsApp Status allows you to share photos, videos, and text updates that disappear after 24 hours. This section provides a step-by-step guide to viewing, posting, and managing WhatsApp Status updates.

## Viewing a Status

1. Open WhatsApp on your laptop.
2. Click Status from the navigation menu.
3. Select the contact's status you want to view.
4. Use the navigation controls to move between status updates.

## Posting a Photo or Video Status

1. Open WhatsApp on your laptop.
2. Click Status.
3. Click Add Status or the option to create a new status.
4. Select Photos & Videos.
5. Choose the photo or video you want to post.
6. Add a caption if needed.
7. Click Send or Post.

## Posting a Text Status

1. Open WhatsApp on your laptop.
2. Click Status.
3. Select the option to create a text status.
4. Enter your message.
5. Format the text or change the background if the available options are displayed.
6. Click Send or Post.

## Replying to a Status

1. Open Status.
2. Select the status you want to reply to.
3. Enter your message in the reply field.
4. Press Enter or click Send.

Your reply will be sent as a private message to the person who posted the status.

## Deleting a Status

1. Open Status.
2. Open My Status.
3. Select the status update you want to delete.
4. Open the More options menu.
5. Select Delete.
6. Confirm the deletion if prompted.

## Checking Status Privacy

Status privacy determines who can view your status updates.

1. Open WhatsApp.
2. Open Settings.
3. Select Privacy.
4. Select Status.
5. Choose the preferred audience for your status updates.

Available options may include My Contacts, My Contacts Except, and Only Share With.
